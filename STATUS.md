## How to read status

- A patch or source change is **not complete** until CI passes on it.
- Passing CI is **not verified on staging or production** until the deployed app is checked.
- Provider integrations (Paystack, Resend, the AI provider, S3, ClamAV, DNS, Turnstile, Expo push, Twilio, Meta) are tested with fakes only. They stay **unverified with live credentials** until each is checked with real credentials (step-20). Staging runs in production mode with `EMAIL_PROVIDER=resend` (API key and from address set), so reset, verification and quotation mails may really be sent; use only your own addresses for test customers. `EMAIL_RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET` is not set, so delivery events cannot be verified.
